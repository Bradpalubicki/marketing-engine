## 1. Industry Overview

### Business Structure
HVAC businesses typically operate as owner-operated or small franchise operations (2-25 technicians). Revenue splits roughly 60% service/repair, 40% installation. Most run 1-3 service vehicles per technician. Key roles: owner/operator, dispatcher, field technicians, office manager.

### Revenue Model
- **Service/Repair calls:** $150–$500 diagnostic + labor + parts. Avg ticket: $200–$500.
- **System installation/replacement:** $3,000–$12,000 depending on unit size, brand, and complexity. Avg: $5,500–$7,500 residential.
- **Maintenance contracts:** $150–$300/yr per unit (2x annual tune-ups). High-margin recurring revenue.
- **Emergency calls:** 1.5–2x standard rate. Premium pricing acceptable to consumers.

### Seasonality
- **Peak demand:** Summer (AC emergency/install), Winter (heating emergency/install)
- **Shoulder season:** Spring (AC pre-season tune-ups), Fall (heating tune-ups)
- **Budget allocation guidance:** 40% summer / 30% winter / 15% spring / 15% fall
- **Emergency demand:** Weather-driven spikes. Cold snap = furnace failures. Heat wave = AC failures. Increase budget 48h before forecast extremes.

### Target Economics
- **Avg service ticket:** $200–$500
- **Avg install ticket:** $3,000–$12,000 (residential); $8,000–$25,000 (light commercial)
- **Target CPA (service):** $80–$180
- **Target CPA (install):** $300–$600
- **Close rate on qualified lead:** 30–50% (service), 20–35% (install)
- **LTV:** $1,200–$3,500 over 5 years (service contract + 1 install cycle)

## 5. Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1: Emergency / Repair (always on, highest priority)
- **Goal:** Calls and form fills for same-day service
- **Budget allocation:** 50% of total budget
- **Ad Groups:**
  - AG1: AC Repair (keywords: ac repair [city], ac not cooling, ac broken)
  - AG2: Furnace Repair (keywords: furnace repair, no heat, furnace not working)
  - AG3: Heat Pump Repair (keywords: heat pump repair, heat pump not working)
  - AG4: Emergency HVAC (keywords: emergency hvac, hvac emergency, 24/7 hvac)
  - AG5: General Repair (keywords: hvac repair near me, hvac technician [city])
- **Match types:** Exact + Phrase ONLY. No broad match. No broad match modifier.
- **Bidding:** Target CPA $80–$180. Start manual CPC if account is new (< 50 conversions/mo).
- **Extensions:** Callout (24/7, licensed, free diagnostic), Call extension (primary number), Sitelinks (Schedule, Reviews, Financing)

### Campaign 2: Installation / Replacement
- **Goal:** Form fills and phone calls for new system quotes
- **Budget allocation:** 35% of total budget
- **Ad Groups:**
  - AG1: AC Installation (new ac, ac installation, central air installation)
  - AG2: Furnace Installation (new furnace, furnace replacement, furnace installation)
  - AG3: Heat Pump Install (heat pump installation, mini split installation)
  - AG4: HVAC System (new hvac system, hvac replacement, hvac system cost)
- **Match types:** Exact + Phrase ONLY.
- **Bidding:** Target CPA $300–$600. Higher CPA acceptable given install revenue.
- **Extensions:** Price extension (starting at $X,XXX), Sitelink (financing options, brands we carry)

### Campaign 3: Maintenance / Tune-Up
- **Goal:** Scheduled appointments for tune-ups and maintenance plans
- **Budget allocation:** 15% of total budget (increase in shoulder season)
- **Ad Groups:**
  - AG1: AC Tune-Up (ac tune up, ac checkup, ac service [city])
  - AG2: Furnace Tune-Up (furnace tune up, furnace checkup, heating service)
  - AG3: Maintenance Plans (hvac maintenance plan, hvac service contract)
- **Match types:** Exact + Phrase ONLY.
- **Bidding:** Target CPA $40–$100. Lower revenue per conversion but high LTV.

**Global settings across all campaigns:**
- Geo-targeting: client's service area zip codes + radius
- Ad scheduling: 24/7 for Emergency, 7am-8pm for Install/Maintenance
- Device: all devices, mobile bid adjustment +20%
- Audience: RLSA (past visitors) with bid adjustment +30%

```

You write ads that generate phone calls and form fills from homeowners experiencing HVAC problems or planning HVAC purchases.

Rules:
- Headlines: max 30 characters including spaces. Count every character.
- Descriptions: max 90 characters including spaces. Count every character.
- Always include urgency or benefit in headline 1.
- Always include a call to action in headline 3 or description.
- Use {city} as a placeholder — the system will replace it with the target city.
- Focus on the customer's pain (hot house, no heat, high bills) not the company's features.
- Never use exclamation marks in headlines (Google policy).
- Use sentence case, not title case.
```

- `{offer}` — primary offer (e.g., "free diagnostic", "$59 tune-up", "0% financing")

## 8. Performance Benchmarks

### Healthy Performance Ranges

| Metric | Target Range | Warning | Critical |
|--------|-------------|---------|----------|
| CTR (search) | 8–14% | < 5% | < 3% |
| Conversion rate (call/form) | 12–20% | < 8% | < 5% |
| CPA — service/repair | $80–$180 | > $200 | > $250 |
| CPA — installation | $300–$600 | > $700 | > $900 |
| Quality Score (avg) | 7–10 | < 6 | < 5 |
| Impression share (search) | > 50% | < 30% | < 20% |

### Red Flags — Investigate Immediately
- CTR < 5%: ad copy mismatch, wrong match type, or poor Quality Score
- CPA > $250 (service): check negative keywords, check LP conversion rate, check call tracking
- Conversion rate < 5%: landing page issue — check page speed, mobile UX, form friction
- Quality Score < 5: landing page relevance or CTR problem — audit ad/LP alignment
- 0 conversions in 7 days with spend: check conversion tracking, check call extension numbers

### Optimization Cadence
- **Daily:** Check for budget pacing, flag anomalies
- **Weekly:** Review search terms report, add negatives, pause underperformers
- **Monthly:** Ad copy rotation analysis, landing page test, bid strategy adjustment
- **Quarterly:** Full account audit, seasonal budget adjustment, competitive landscape review
